Morgenröte – Dawn of Flavor is a Mod to enrich the world of Victoria 3 with culture, beauty and lifestyle. We want to improve the cultural and scientific aspects of the Victorian century that only appear briefly in the previously sterile game world. These include immersive scientific expeditions, increasing prestige through culture, and training artists and scientists.

[img]https://i.imgur.com/rkNmG4P.png[/img]
[h1] The Project [/h1]
 [list]
    [*]Scientists (“academics”) such as archaeologists, anthropologists, naturalists, paleontologists, astronomers and engineers, including a scientific society. These academics not only have special skills to revolutionize 19th century science, but can change the academic worldview at scientific conferences.
    [*]Artists such as composers, painters and writers who can turn even small nations into cultural centers. Nations adopt certain cultural traditions, build galleries or promote the book industry, and try to win international prizes.
    [*]Other personalities from society and sport, such as balloonists, pilots or athletes, who gain international reputation. The Olympic Games were added, aviation was significantly expanded, whether with airports, new mobilization options, giant airships or the legendary Schneider Trophy.
[/list]

[h1] Compatibility [/h1]
Although we try to touch as few vanilla files as necessary, we still need to overwrite or inject into some of the files. This applies particularily to various technologies, buildings, pop needs and buy packages. Mods that also modify those files might need a compatch.

[b] Morgenröte can not be added to a already existing game. You need to start a new game with it. [/b]

[h1] Information for Creators [/h1]
The music Morgenröte adds is in the public domain in Europe. Sadly some other countries seem to not follow the same rules. So, if you want to monetize your video, we recommend to turn the Morgenröte Music off.
