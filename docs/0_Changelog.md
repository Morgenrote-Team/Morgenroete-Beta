Changelog fot 2.9.2 "Zeppelin"

Zeppelin - General
-

Zeppelin - Expedition
- Arctic Expedition is now an Action in the Zeppelin Captain GUI. The old Zeppelin Expedition under Scientific Expeditions has been replaced with the Deep Sea Expedition (Coming soon).
- Arctic Expedition is now some kind of mix between the old event driven system and the new event log system from the Schneider Trophy: Progress/Danger Events are now handled by the log, but bonus events are still there. You'll encounter 1-5 events per expedition.
- Arctic Expedition lenght is based on Zeppelin range. The more range you have, the longer takes the expedition and you may get more rewards.
- Zeppelin Danger comes with negative log events. All these events can be prevented by technology or Zeppelin Modules. Be careful.
- No more Zeppelin rescue missions. Either you succeed, or the mission ends with the destruction of your Zeppelin and the death of the Zeppelin captain.
- Narhwal can be documented now.
- Polar Bear can be documented now.
- Polar Bear can only be catched with a Polar Bear Cage.
- You can chose a nature, physics or no focus for an expedition. Focus needs a naturalist or a physicist.
- One of the main goals is the mapping of Antarctica. There are eight region + the north Pole. After you mapped 8 regions, you get the crossing of the North Pole. First nation doing this gets a pretsige bonus.
- Depending on modules and range, a zeppelin can get 1-3 arctic rgeions per trip.
- The Zeppelin GUI includes a map of the Arctic that shows which regions you have already explored and which you haven't.
- The four discoveries in the Arctic—geographical, zoological, paleontological, and physical—are highlighted in the Arctic GUI.
